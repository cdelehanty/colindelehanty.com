    ## iPhone and iPad Launch Campaigns

</MDXContainer>

<MDXContainer containerType="text">
	Through Magnit Global, I worked as a contract photographer supporting Apple
	Inc. on multiple productions, capturing media shot on pre-release versions of
	the iPhone and iPad to demonstrate the capabilities of their new hardware for
	product launches. A highlight from these productions was the selection of three
	time-lapses to showcase the new time-lapse feature introduced with iOS 8,
	first on the iPhone 6 (Sept 2014) and later on the iPad Air 2 (Oct 2014). The
	time-lapses were featured in product announcements, digital media, and
	in-store demos, receiving positive feedback from Apple's top executives.
</MDXContainer>
